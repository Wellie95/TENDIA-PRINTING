Frequently asked
questions.

Quick answers before you place an order.

Can I bring my own artwork?

Yes. Send your artwork and we can check whether it is suitable for the requested print size and method. Artwork adjustments may be quoted separately.

Can you help if I do not have artwork?

Yes. Tell us what you want to print and we can discuss the design requirements and prepare artwork as a separate design service where needed.

Do you handle bulk apparel orders?

Yes. T-shirts, hoodies, workwear and team apparel can be quoted according to garment type, quantity, print size and artwork.

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote form below or contact TENDIA PRINTING on WhatsApp at 075 443 9922. Include the service, quantity, size and deadline for a faster response.
